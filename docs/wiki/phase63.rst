========================================

native evaluation views. The failed masking attempt was repaired before any
optimizer update; its replacement preserves the original scientific design.

Neither arm passes every original gate. Recovery fails half-source precision
and half-perfect LCAG; masking still fails half-perfect LCAG. All exact primary
components have one mother and depth one. Generated roots and depth do not
establish correct recursive reconstruction. No exact component deeper than one
appears across all checkpoint views and returned candidates. Both masking
forest successes contain no mothers; recovery has one forest with one shallow
mother and one without mothers.

Masking improves aggregate retained source scores, which include isolated leaves.
For nontrivial full components, recall is instead 230/728 versus 225/728.
Paired event-bootstrap intervals span zero for topology, coherent forests and
nontrivial source metrics. The single-seed exploratory comparison establishes
neither a recursive quality winner nor a causal improvement over earlier phases.

Masking is the simpler proposed baseline for a separate radial-projection
reconditioning study. Post-training geometry diagnostics and the exact
intervention must precede a frozen study contract. Hold training size and search
limits fixed. No successor, checkpoint promotion or sealed-test access follows
automatically. The completed comparison leaves 46700 validation events unreserved.
