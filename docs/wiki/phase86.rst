Phase86 structural reachability and repository cleanup
======================================================

Phase86 completed a diagnostic on 96 previously inspected TRAIN collisions,
16 in each of six categories. **No contrast was admitted and no performance
improvement was established.** The frozen source-set gates passed, but each
candidate branch had only four deep fit targets compatible with reconstructed
intrinsic daughter height, below the original five-target support intention.
Height compatibility is necessary, not sufficient: equal source sets can have
different recursive daughter topology or PID. The frozen gates were not rewritten.

All 455 eligible mothers remain in the primary denominator; 210 outside-policy
targets remain separate. The largest mutually exclusive source-set diagnoses
were 154 wrong irreversible merges and 147 pointer-ranking or source-selection
failures. Across all rounds, 254 targets had a policy-valid source group with
correct mother type. Immediate daughter source groups were generated for 41
and accepted for 36; adding correct mother type gives 40 and 35 respectively.
These are not verified recursive physical-tree reconstructions.

Whole-set generation had 123 supported fit targets of 278, with 12 source-only
deep fit and 18 deep assessment witnesses. Necessary-height support reduced
the deep counts to four fit and three assessment. Split/delay repair had 80
supported failed fit targets of 254, with 12 and 17 source-only deep witnesses,
reduced to four and four. Repair witnesses are oracle opportunity bounds,
not deployable gains. No structural repair, component-weight contrast,
normalization refit, training update or fresh development evaluation followed.

Evaluation scope
----------------

Exact and inclusive B membership recovery were both zero of 64 B trials and
zero of 32 B collision events. Continuum fake-B acceptance was zero of 64
collision events. All 64 B channel bins had one evaluated trial, zero correct
and zero unavailable. These retained, reduced-PID proxies are not FEI physical
efficiency. The singleton greedy pool is not a beam-search comparison.
Each category has only 16 events, far below the complete-evaluation requirement
of 2,000 per category. No independent generalization inference follows.

All 96 committed event states were valid, acyclic, single-parent and recursively
source-disjoint. Daughter-sum closure passed for all 336 mothers, and all 3,879
input sources were covered. However, 28 events retained disconnected input
resource aliases: full forest-root disjointness passed for only 68 of 96.
Closure is an implementation invariant, not physical momentum resolution.

Repository cleanup
------------------

Lossless preservation preceded removal of 17,199 inactive cache files and
1,787 generated documentation files. Three generated files totaling
407,854,100 bytes (about 408 MB) migrated to external storage. Another 230 logs
were packed and 1,225 existing logs indexed; raw evidence and legacy log
originals remain retained. Portable restore tooling verifies hashes and refuses
conflicting destinations. Private archives and verification data stay on the
data volume and are not mirrored to Git or this site.

The selected home files decreased by **941,618,410 logical bytes** and
**254,145,536 filesystem-reported allocated bytes**. These are distinct
measurements, not claims about Git-history shrinkage or measured account quota.
No scientific source or model behavior changed in the cleanup.
