----------------------------------

Hypothesis A is that the learned mother-type and daughter compatibility prior improves legal recursive assembly; hypothesis B is that it misdirects early merges and removing it helps. The modern sparse deep candidate support makes decoder use of representations a more informative next diagnostic than a third hybrid confirmation.
