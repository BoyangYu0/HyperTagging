Earlier studies
===============

The :doc:`dashboard <_generated/status/index>` collects earlier study decisions
in one summary and expands only the latest completed study. Historical metrics
retain their original populations and definitions; differences across cohorts
are not controlled comparisons. Newly added tagging metrics remain unavailable
for studies that did not record them.

Historical conclusions at a glance
----------------------------------

- **Phase78–83:** probes, optimizer changes and pair supervision did not
  demonstrate membership improvement on the inspected training-role panels.
  Phase81 remained at 0/1024 main exact memberships in both arms; Phase83
  passed none of twelve structural improvement gates. See :doc:`previous_studies`.
- **Phase84–85:** small-pool TRAIN fitting improved, but both pair-connection
  arms remained at 0/400 raw and accepted source sets on fresh development.
  This did not establish generalization or justify scale-up. See :doc:`phase84_85`.
- **Phase86:** source-set support checks passed, but necessary deep support
  was insufficient to admit a contrast. This was a reachability diagnosis,
  not new training or a measured gain. See :doc:`phase86`.
- **CR032:** immutable charge obstructed 14/64 nominal B groups, limiting
  accepted recovery to at most 50/64 against the unchanged 61/64 requirement.
  The remaining groups were not proved achievable. See :doc:`cr032`.

These are historical conclusions on distinct populations, not a statement
about later unpublished research. Exact source-set recovery does not establish
recursive topology or PID recovery. Full evidence and denominators remain in
the linked reports and downloads.
