Earlier studies
===============

The :doc:`dashboard <_generated/status/index>` collects earlier study decisions
in one summary and expands only the latest completed study. Historical metrics
retain their original populations and definitions; differences across cohorts
are not controlled comparisons. Newly added tagging metrics remain unavailable
for studies that did not record them.
