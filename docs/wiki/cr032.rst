CR032: a fixed acceptance gate is infeasible
============================================

Reviewed 15 October 2026. The fixed native TRAIN panel cannot meet its
unchanged accepted-membership gate under the existing target and decoder
contracts. **Fourteen of 64 nominal B groups have incompatible immutable
detector charge. Accepted recovery is therefore at most 50/64, below the
required 61/64.** This is a feasibility finding, not a reconstruction gain.
Observed archived mixed recovery remains 1/64 raw and accepted.

Scope and necessary bound
-------------------------

CR032 audited exactly 32 previously used TRAIN collisions: 16 charged and
16 mixed, contributing 64 nominal B trials. All 64 were processed; failed
and membership-unavailable counts are both zero. Every original trial remains
in the denominator. Scientific source is
``6e1b5bd4a34a9a70174baf1099a01847ba7c0a1f``.

Persistent composite charge is the exact daughter sum. Across every allowed
mother and all six rounds, accepted mother charges are restricted to
{-1, 0, +1}. Fourteen target source groups have immutable charge outside that
union: charge -3 for two groups, -2 for five, +2 for six and +4 for one.
All 64 charge certificates are available. Missing identities, capacity,
singleton and within-group alias checks certify no additional obstruction;
cross-unit alias checks are false for all 32 events. Optional unavailable
certificates remain optimistic, not measured failures. No archived positive
witness is certified impossible.

Raw bounds omit accepted charge constraints. The remaining 50 groups are
**not proved achievable**: source-set and charge compatibility establish
neither legal decoder reachability nor recursive topology/PID correctness.
Physical mass/PID optimization is unavailable. Intrinsic target height and
decoder round remain distinct. Whole-event bounds preserve joint source
ownership, including retained internal composites. Certificates are never
counted as reconstructed tags.

Earlier flat studies had already identified charge incompatibility on their
own panels. CR032 establishes the consequence for this unchanged native gate;
it does not rename the earlier observation as a new learning method.

Operations, evidence and limitations
------------------------------------

CR032 performed zero updates, checkpoint loads, model calls, rollouts or new
candidate generation. It consumed all 32 selected payloads and 32 archive
rows, transported within 96 archive rows. Consumer wall time was 69.510 s,
CPU time 71.566 s and maximum resident memory 995,680 KiB. Allocation elapsed
was 98.030 s. The allocation used two CPUs, no GPU, 32 GiB memory and an
eight-hour ceiling without automatic requeue. Independent review recomputed
all 64 certificates and found no positive-witness contradiction.

CR009 completed both fits after one failed mixed preparation attempt. The
failed attempt remains in resource accounting. Completed mixed and generic
allocations elapsed 3:58:48 and 3:50:36, with CPU totals 7:28:15 and 7:05:16;
peak monitored memory was 5.342 and 9.080 GiB. Its training/evaluation resource
and exposure tables remain separate from the zero-fitting CR032 audit.

A documented CR009 telemetry classification correction separates assessment
calls accidentally counted again in the PID-diagnostic subset. Raw subset
receipts retain 4,293 model calls, 8,586 encoder calls and 22,248 presentations;
the corrected training-only totals are 2,565, 5,130 and 20,520. The difference
is the assessment workload. Overall counts, optimizer behavior and scientific
outcomes are unaffected; no model or bootstrap was rerun. Downloads preserve
both raw accounting and its explicit correction.

CR032 supplies no new tagging, deep-proposal, round-specific, continuum or
recursive-topology performance. The inherited CR027 shared evaluation is
archived control evidence, not new CR032 performance. The fixed-panel logical
ceiling is exact for its assumptions; it is not a corpus-wide prevalence
estimate. Neither study establishes physical full-tree or FEI-equivalent
efficiency, complete post-study coverage, fresh-data improvement or robustness
across seeds.

The :doc:`metric catalogue <_generated/status/downloads>` provides complete
privacy-safe scoped metrics, exact saved precision, availability fields,
source hashes and a decoder. Event identities, private unit proofs, machine
locations, raw payloads and checkpoints remain outside the public artifact.
The :doc:`Phase84–85 <phase84_85>` and :doc:`Phase86 <phase86>` releases and
their downloads remain unchanged, including the historical unfinished CR001
infrastructure cutoff.

Consequence for the campaign
----------------------------

Further parameter, loss or threshold trials aimed solely at passing this
impossible fixed-panel accepted gate are not justified. The next design
review should examine the retained-target and source-provenance contract
prospectively, preserving all 64 nominal trials and the physics constraints.
The observed 1/64 remains far below the optimistic 50/64 ceiling; the audit
does not explain that remaining learning gap. A measured learning mechanism
may still justify future training. This publication admits no revised gate,
easier subset, new cohort, corpus expansion, model promotion or new study.
