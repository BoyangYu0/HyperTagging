Decision across all studies
--------------------------------------------------

* Early pretraining, capacity and Stage A studies established software/runtime contracts, not promotion-grade physics. Their legacy edge/tree metrics are not comparable to strict full-decay metrics. Phase34 compared steps 54,064/81,096/108,128; two fresh confirmations did not establish a primary advantage for the earlier checkpoint. Phase35 frozen/late adaptation tied under an older decoder. Phases36–39 improved local pointer/recovery metrics with sparse exact hierarchies.
* Phase40 doubled 35,000 to 70,000 training events while doubling steps and changing the validation cohort. It is not a controlled learning curve. Phase41 pointer reduction worsened recovery; Phase42 longer pretraining (108,128 versus 81,096) did not improve its primary or pass all gates.
* Phase43 had a small adaptation signal, reversed in seed-deviating Phase44 diagnostics. Phase45 higher late encoder learning rate tied and predates corrected scientific source. Corrected Phases46/47 were mixed. Phase48 PID adaptation never executed because of the reconstruction autocast-cache defect; corrected Phases49–51 did not establish a replicated quality gain. This downstream defect is not evidence of a pretraining-loop defect.
* Recovery doses 52–54 did not produce a reproducible joint topology/forest gain. Phase53 did exactly recover one shared depth-two continuum component, not a coherent forest or a treatment advantage. Phase55 parent-weight effects were shallow, mixed and runtime-sensitive; failed controls 56/58 prevented matched conclusions. Phase57 strict-event contamination invalidates independent quality claims.
* Phase59 completed both arms but was a 25-event feasibility study. Phase60 failed identically before attempted step 547 (PID/LCA 28.020851>20), before the late treatment or validation. Phase61 reused its unscored reservation and seed with adaptive shared early PID 0.5; it establishes completion at this seed, with mixed shallow downstream evidence.

Current transfer coverage is complete: both arms load all 121 encoder keys and both PID-head keys, with zero missing, unexpected or shape-mismatched keys. This establishes parameter coverage, not useful learned representation. Reconstruction freezes PID and adapts the encoder after step 2188. Both start from the same historical step 81096 parameters with fresh train-only normalization, optimizer, schedule, RNG and memory.

**Training size:** hold 70,000 now. No controlled result establishes immediate data limitation; more data could still help. **Pretraining:** prioritize objective/representation quality and execution stability, not simply longer duration. Its superiority over training-data growth has not been tested head-to-head. Target-policy incompatibility and weak recursive assembly also need to be distinguished from representation quality.

**Validation:** the earlier 50,000 pool was exhausted, and Phase60 added 50,000 source-disjoint validation events without growing training data. Phase61 scored its 1,000 selection plus 100 strict reservation. Fresh validation is necessary for the next comparison, but another source expansion is unnecessary: 48,900 remain before Phase62.
