# Phase64: train-calibrated radial reconditioning

The next study tests a single initialization intervention: rescale the shared
hyperbolic projection weight and bias once, then perform equal pretraining
refinement and identical masking-only reconstruction. The control receives an
unchanged parameter-only initialization. This is an exploratory mechanism and
quality comparison, not a promoted model or proof that hyperbolic geometry is
beneficial.

## Evidence and calibration

Phase63 did not repair radial saturation. On 32 previously used Phase63 selection
events, every measured node in both selected reconstruction encoders has a radial
cap derivative below 1e-4. Median pre-cap norms are about 40–47 against a cap of
1.5; mean derivatives are approximately 4e-8 to 8e-8. These diagnostics use the
same frozen pretraining runtime PID/input policy for all encoders. They are not
strict rollout measurements or population-wide estimates.

The calibration sample contains 128 events selected by deterministic hash ranking
from the existing 70,000 training events. No validation value fits the scale.
The unchanged initial encoder's FSP-view median norm is 44.285671; the rule
`min(1, 0.5 * cap / training_median)` yields **0.016935500334518513**. This is one
predeclared rule, not a scale sweep. It targets half the cap for a fixed projection
input. Because the encoder uses hyperbolic relations internally, changing the
projection also changes upstream context: actual post-intervention median norms
are approximately 0.82–0.92 on training views, rather than exactly 0.75.

After rescaling, the measured saturated fraction is zero in every training and
development view, with mean radial derivatives 0.67–0.72. These mechanism checks
support running the experiment, but do not establish stable gradients after
refinement or improved reconstruction. All measured derivatives and per-level
radius variances, cohort UIDs, source hashes and diagnostic provenance are in
`artifacts/codex/phase64_radial_diagnostics_20260930.json`. Native diagnostics and
the exact diagnostic script snapshot remain on the data volume under
`HyperTagging_artifacts/phase64_review_20260930`.

Both warm starts discard optimizer, schedule and RNG state, and preserve the
fresh train-fitted normalization and memory buffers. The derived initialization
artifact is explicitly parameter-only, contains provenance, and cannot overwrite
the source. Its transformation and hash are recorded in each run's refinement
receipt. The architecture, cap function, loss kernels and inference policy are
unchanged. The projection direction is preserved for a fixed input, but complete
encoder outputs need not retain direction because context changes.

Both arms use the phase-weighted validation objective for pretraining diagnostics;
the encoder transferred to reconstruction is always the fixed final step 2,188,
not the best validation checkpoint. Reconstruction selects only on the reserved
1,000 events. The strict 100 and its fixed 20-event beam subset are separate.
All 53,300 historical reservations are excluded, leaving 45,600 validation events
unreserved. Sealed test remains closed.

The Phase63 choice of masking is a complexity baseline: its exact full components
tied recovery, and both its coherent forests contained no mothers. It is not a
proven quality winner. Phase64 does not simultaneously alter recovery, parent/PID
weights, training size, capacities, thresholds, search rankers or beam budgets.
Additional refinement occurs equally in both arms; a cross-phase comparison to
Phase63 cannot isolate the rescaling effect.

## Success, failure and follow-up

Review post-refinement radial derivatives, radius spread by supported level,
finite objective gradients and unchanged dominance guards. Initialization alone
cannot establish that saturation stays repaired. The control may remain
saturated; that is an outcome of the controlled comparison, not grounds to stop
it selectively or relax its guards.

Then validate all original reconstruction gates, all fourteen native reports,
all returned candidates in full/half scopes, exact repeats, and paired event
uncertainty. Separate isolated leaves from nontrivial components; report exact
components by depth and coherent forests by mother count and B-partition status.
More radial variance, generated depth or root output alone is insufficient.
A useful outcome needs joint improvement in nontrivial recursive quality and
source precision/recall while satisfying the original gates.

If the projection re-saturates or objective stability fails, preserve the native
failure and diagnose the mechanism before another experiment. No automatic
restart, successor, longer budget, checkpoint promotion or sealed-test access
is part of this study. Independent training-seed replication remains necessary
before a quality claim.
