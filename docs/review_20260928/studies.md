## Assessment

The current program has substantially improved correctness, source separation, cohort discipline, reproducibility metadata and loss-execution checking. It has not demonstrated useful full-decay reconstruction, a reliable hyperbolic-pretraining advantage, or an established best loss schedule. Holding the 70,000-event training set while resolving these questions is reasonable. Repeated small adjustments to loss weights are approaching diminishing scientific value; after the already submitted Phase62 replication, the next study should diagnose target representability, representation transfer and recursive assembly separately.

The critical distinction is between reconstructing a detector-source set plus its mother's PID and reconstructing the recursively correct decay topology. The historical checkpoint-selection metric mostly measures the former. Phase61 reports approximately 9.2% source-set-plus-mother-PID recovery but only 0.79% retained full LCAG pair accuracy, zero coherent primary forests in 100 strict events, and exclusively depth-one exact components. Passing the original numerical gates is not sufficient evidence to promote a full-decay model. Evidence: `docs/wiki/phase61.rst:6`, `:79`, `:193`, `:209`, `:281`; metric distinction in `docs/audits/current_status.md:378`.

## Scientific goal and what is actually established

The intended scientific goal is detector-only, source-exclusive, hierarchical reconstruction of complete or explicitly retained decay structures, with PID and physically consistent composite construction. The architecture is a plausible inductive bias: detector adapters, contextual/hyperbolic representation learning, then level-wise mother sets and pointers. Scientific success must include recursive topology and source/PID conjunctions, rather than correct tree syntax, large retained-input coverage or exact daughter-sum closure. Hyperbolic geometry's incremental downstream value remains unproven in the recorded studies; execution of an encoder or low weighted objective is not such evidence.

The preserved migration phases 1–13 concern software equivalence and packaging, not thirteen physics studies. They migrate utilities, provisional data contracts, legacy subprocess adapters, distinct losses, models, dry-run training, single-step reconstruction, link modes, GraFEI full evaluation, GPT-like helpers and examples. Historical GraFEI padded PID accuracy can exceed one; historical GPT MultiGPT branches are ambiguous. Their metrics must not be compared numerically to the present strict evaluator. Evidence: `docs/historical_migration_provenance.md:15`, `:65`, `:130`, `:144`, `:202`.

## Comparability and independence audit

1. Keep four evidence boundaries separate: legacy metrics; strict original complete-only policy; expanded retained components; contracted and oracle diagnostics. A source-set match, a configured root, an exact LCAG component, a coherent forest and a topology-plus-all-PID success are different endpoints.
2. Seven report views per arm are checkpoint tracks/diagnostics/repeats, not seven trained models. Event/component/pair counts differ; compute micro rates from summed counts and publish macro rates separately. Isolated leaves must not inflate nontrivial exactness.
3. Phases40 and42 do not prove data cannot help or all pretraining is useless.40 confounds data and compute;42 evaluates longer historical pretraining through a particular decoder.55–62 compare two refinement schedules, not refinement versus no refinement, and not hyperbolic versus Euclidean representations.
4. Phase44 is seed-deviating post-hoc evidence;45 precedes corrected source;48 did not execute treatment;56/58 have missing controls;57 inspected strict events during selection;59 has only25 independent strict events;60 has no endpoints;61 is an adaptive stability pilot. These should be flags in a machine ledger, not prose a reader must rediscover.
5. Phase61 matched configured seeds do not imply identical precontrast execution:123/141 tensors differ at step1,094; first logged loss divergence944. Phase62 explicitly preserves this variation rather than changing kernels or branching from common full state. This does not make randomized schedule replication invalid, but a single paired contrast mixes intervention and runtime variation, and cannot isolate a tiny treatment effect. `configs/reconstruction/ht_reconstruction_phase62_20260927.json:10070`.
6. The old50,000 validation pool is exhausted. Phase60 added50,000 source-disjoint events. Phase62 excludes all51,100 previously used/reserved validation UIDs, reserves1,000 selection+100 strict with20 beam subset, excludes99,000 non-selection UIDs before training, leaving47,800 unreserved. Independence is event/seed independence inside a fixed source pool, not new source-domain generalization. `docs/wiki/phase61.rst:517`, `:522`.

## Recommended next scientific procedure

**Complete and classify the already submitted Phase62 without changing its contract.** Confirm native execution, treatment gradients, selector membership, checkpoint lineage, full/half metrics and all candidates. Compare only the paired arms on its own untouched cohort. Preserve incomplete/missing outcomes. Do not label it a winner solely because a gate passed or one sparse component improved.

**Then replace dose chasing with failure decomposition.** On train/development diagnostics, measure per-depth target representability; teacher-state daughter/PID accuracy; predicted-state representability; proposal recall before constraints, after constraints and after pruning; and same-event top1 versus coherent oracle@K. Separate missing proposals, incorrect scoring, incompatible ontology/unary/charge policy, and compounding assembly failures. These diagnostics identify whether preprocessing, representation, loss or search is the limiting stage. Preserve unconditional physics denominators alongside representable-only diagnostic slices.

**Make a controlled representation baseline.** Compare random initialization versus current frozen/adapted encoder and Euclidean versus hyperbolic geometry under equal downstream compute, matched parameter capacity and a frozen target/evaluation contract. A refinement/no-refinement control is needed before saying the new pretraining improves reconstruction. Then ablate parent/tree geometry, PID and channel losses one factor at a time, choosing a small preregistered primary topology endpoint. Current parent-ranking accuracy~0.729 and effective rank~24 certify neither useful hyperbolic geometry nor transfer. Final phase61 radius/level correlation near zero merits targeted diagnostic assessment, with signs and heterogeneous populations checked before interpreting it as failure.

**For the next small-effect paired experiment, share and hash the full pre-intervention state.** Fork both arms from a common checkpoint including optimizer, scheduler, RNG, data cursor and memory; record hardware/library/precision and log input UID/order digests. Test a no-treatment duplicate run to measure the noise floor. Use multiple independent seeds to estimate training variation. Do not retrofit this into current immutable Phase62 outcomes.

**Redesign the promotion endpoint to match the goal.** Preserve historical gates for comparability, but require predeclared nontrivial recursive topology, topology+all-PID, depth≥2, and relevant B-pair/category endpoints on adequate independent events, with feasibility/representability explicitly reported. A coherent forest consisting entirely of leaves is not a success for decay reconstruction. Keep performance/throughput and calibrated score quality separate from topology.

**Use validation efficiently.** Keep a reusable development cohort for debugging/ablations, a registry of touched UIDs, and a fresh confirmation cohort used once only after the candidate and endpoint are locked. Large exhaustive per-tree exports are valuable private audit material, but public/current status should carry compact counts, uncertainties and named evidence links. Reserve a source-domain/generalization cohort separately; the sealed test remains outside tuning.

**Revisit scaling only after the error decomposition.** Run a controlled35k/70k/140k learning curve with both equal-compute and equal-exposure comparisons, fixed category mixture and evaluation cohort, once a useful recursive endpoint exists. Existing studies neither establish an immediate data bottleneck nor rule one out.
